Wabi AI Features, Explained

Multi-thread organisation

Instead of one infinite conversation, Wabi splits your life into threads — one per topic, project or group of people. Each thread keeps its own history and tools, which the founder argues keeps long-term context from degrading the way single-thread chatbots do.

Multiplayer threads (group chats with an AI inside)

Share a thread with your partner, family or friends. Everyone can talk to Wabi, answer its questions, and use the mini-apps in that thread — planning a trip or running a household becomes a shared activity instead of five separate chats with five separate assistants.

Mini-apps, generated in the chat

Wabi's headline feature: describe a small tool and it builds it inside the conversation — trackers, polls, itineraries, lists, quizzes, countdowns. In version 2.0 the agent also decides on its own which apps to create, starting from the moment you connect your email. Wabi calls this category “personal software”: apps that are disposable, personal and remixable rather than downloaded from a store.

Home-screen widgets

Mini-apps and live info can be added to your iOS or Android home screen as widgets — a trip countdown, today's priorities, a habit tracker — without opening the app.

Connections: email, calendar, bank

To act proactively, Wabi can connect to your email, calendars and — optionally — payment methods or bank accounts. Access is scoped per chat: a family thread doesn't automatically see what a finance thread sees, and members of a chat can see when personal information is requested inside it. Details and cautions on the safety page.

Errands, handled

Wabi's store listing is explicit about legwork: “Book an appointment. Handle a return. Find a refund. Cancel a subscription.” It can also watch for a bill that changed or a refund that's still missing and start a thread about it. Wabi says it checks with you before important actions that need your approval.

Health & fitness summaries (Android)

On Android you can optionally connect Health Connect — read-only — and ask Wabi about your steps, active calories, distance, workouts, sleep, heart rate and weight, with weekly reviews and comparisons in chat. Wabi's own wording: for fitness and wellness awareness, not medical diagnosis or treatment. The official store descriptions also mention connections for contacts, location and photos, and — on Android — starting voice or video calls from a chat.

Cross-platform

Start on your Mac, pick up on your iPhone or Android phone. Wabi is available on iOS, Android and Mac, and the service is currently free.

Under the hood

Wabi runs in the cloud. Launch coverage notes that requests and connected data may be processed by models from Google, Anthropic and OpenAI, depending on the task. The company states it is SOC 2 compliant, with most user data stored in the United States.
