Wabi AI Download (iOS, Android & Mac)

Wabi AI is available on iOS, Android and Mac. The safest route is always the official website, wabi.ai, which links to the current store listings. Here are the steps for each platform.

iPhone (iOS)

  • Open wabi.ai in Safari and tap the iOS download link, or open the App Store and search for Wabi: AI Assistant — or jump straight to the official App Store listing.
  • Check the provider name: it must say Wabi, Inc.
  • Tap Get — the app is free. Requirements per the App Store: iPhone only (no separate iPad app) and iOS 26.0 or later. The listing currently declares English as its language.

Android

  • Open wabi.ai and tap the Google Play link, or search Wabi in the Play Store — the Android app is listed as “Wabi: AI Messenger”.
  • Confirm the developer is Wabi, Inc. before installing. The app is free and contains no ads.

Mac

  • Go to wabi.ai and use the Mac download link provided there.
  • Start on your computer and pick up on your phone — Wabi syncs across your devices.
⚠️ Don't download the wrong “Wabi”. The app stores contain several unrelated apps with similar names:
  • Wabi – Virtual Phone Number (for WhatsApp Business) — a different company, not the AI assistant.
  • WABI car subscription (Santander, Spain) — a car rental service, unrelated.
  • Waabi — a self-driving technology company (note the double “a”).
If the developer isn't Wabi, Inc., it's not the app in this guide.

Is there a Wabi AI for Windows or Web?

As of October 2026, no Windows app has been announced, and there is no official web version. Be suspicious of any site offering a “Wabi AI for PC” download file — unofficial installers are a common malware vector. We'll update this page if an official Windows version is announced.
