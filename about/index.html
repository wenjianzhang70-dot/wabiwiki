About Wabi Wiki

By Wabi Wiki Editorial · Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Wabi Wiki is an unofficial, fan-made guide to the Wabi AI assistant. When a brand-new app launches, the first questions people search — what is it, where do I download it, is it safe, why won't it log in — are usually answered only by scattered news articles. This site exists to answer them in one place, clearly and in order.

Short answer

Wabi Wiki is an unofficial, fan-made guide to the Wabi AI assistant. It gathers answers about what Wabi is, how to download and use it, safety and troubleshooting in one place. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Wabi, Inc.

How we work

  • Verified facts only. Content is based on Wabi's official site and reputable launch coverage (TechCrunch, Unite.AI and similar), with dates shown. If something isn't confirmed, we say so instead of guessing.
  • No fake hands-on claims. Where a page is based on announcements rather than long-term testing, it says so.
  • Kept current. We follow Wabi's releases on the updates page and revise guides as the product changes.

How we research

We source information from Wabi's official website, official app-store listings and reputable media coverage. We verify facts individually and note the last verified date on each page.

Not affiliated

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Wabi, Inc. “Wabi” is a trademark of its respective owner. For the product itself and anything account-specific, go to the official site: wabi.ai.
