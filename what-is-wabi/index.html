What Is Wabi AI, Really?

By Wabi Wiki Editorial · Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Short answer

Wabi AI is a personal AI assistant designed to be used with other people. It keeps separate threads for different topics and goals, and builds mini-apps such as trackers, polls and itineraries inside each thread. Wabi was founded by Eugenia Kuyda and is currently free.

Wabi AI is a personal AI assistant designed to be used with other people — not just by yourself. It works like a messaging app: you keep separate threads for different topics and goals (health, family, a trip, a shared project), and inside each thread the assistant can build mini-apps on the fly — a weight tracker, a kids'-events finder, a voting poll, a trip itinerary — instead of only replying with text.

How Wabi is different from a normal chatbot

  • Many threads, not one endless chat. Each topic keeps its own context, so yesterday's grocery list doesn't bury today's travel plan.
  • Multiplayer by design. Threads can be shared as group chats. Everyone in the thread can ask Wabi things and use the mini-apps it builds.
  • It builds software, not just answers. Wabi calls this “personal software”: small, disposable apps generated from a conversation, which you can also pin to your phone's home screen as widgets.
  • Proactive help. Connect your email and Wabi automatically sets up starter threads and apps; connect a calendar and it can keep plans moving in the background.

Who created Wabi?

Wabi was founded by Eugenia Kuyda. A former culture journalist in Moscow, she founded the conversational-AI startup Luka, created a memorial chatbot that evolved into Replika (launched in 2016, one of the best-known AI companion apps), and left Replika in 2025 to build Wabi. The company behind the app is Wabi, Inc., backed by a $20 million pre-seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), announced on November 5, 2025.

A short history

  • 2025 — the first Wabi. The original product let you create mini-apps by describing them in text and share them with friends.
  • September 28, 2026 — Wabi 2.0, invite-only. A relaunch as a personal AI messenger: now the agent decides which apps to build for you, and threads became multiplayer.
  • October 19, 2026 — public launch. Wabi 2.0 opened to everyone on iOS, Android and Mac, free, with no waitlist.

Is Wabi free?

Yes — for now. At launch, Wabi is completely free to download and use. The company has openly said it hasn't settled on a business model yet, so pricing may appear later. We track changes on the updates page.

“Wabi 2.0” or “Wabi 3.0”? The version confusion, cleared up

The product generation is called Wabi 2.0 — that's how the company and all launch coverage describe it. But if you open the App Store you'll see version numbers like 3.0.5: the iOS app's build numbering simply runs on a different track (the September 28, 2026 update was iOS version 3.0.0; Android is at 2.0.0). Same product — don't let the numbers confuse you, and be wary of any site selling a separate “Wabi 3.0” product.
