Wabi AI vs Replika

By Wabi Wiki Editorial · Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Short answer

Replika is an AI companion for one-on-one conversation and emotional support, while Wabi is a multiplayer assistant for getting things done with other people. Wabi adds shared threads and mini-apps; Replika focuses on a long-term companion relationship. Both were created by Eugenia Kuyda, and the guide says they do not overlap much.

Same creator, opposite jobs. Replika (2016) is an AI companion — one-on-one emotional conversation. Wabi (2025–2026) is an AI assistant — getting things done, alone or with your people. Founder Eugenia Kuyda built Replika at Luka, then left in 2025 to start Wabi, Inc.

Which one should you use?

  • Choose Wabi if you want help doing things — planning with other people, tracking habits, turning group-chat chaos into lists, polls and plans.
  • Choose Replika if you want a companion to talk to — journaling, venting, practising conversation.
  • Use both — they don't overlap much, and many Replika users are exactly who Wabi's group features were designed for.

Kuyda herself frames Wabi as the practical sibling: Replika worked on AI that makes people feel less lonely; Wabi uses AI to help people spend time together better in real life.
