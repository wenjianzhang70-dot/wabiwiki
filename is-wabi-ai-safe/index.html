Is Wabi AI Safe? The Privacy Facts

Short answer: Wabi is a legitimate, venture-backed app from a known founder, and it was designed with per-chat permission controls — but it asks for unusually deep access (email, calendar, even banking) to deliver its proactive features. Here is what is verified, and the precautions we'd take.

What Wabi can access — only what you connect

  • Email — used to auto-create starter threads and apps, and for briefs and triage.
  • Calendar — used for scheduling and shared plans.
  • Payment method / bank account — optional, for tasks you ask it to handle in a chat.
  • Health data (Android) — optional, read-only Health Connect access to steps, sleep, heart rate, workouts and weight, per the Google Play listing. The store descriptions also list contacts, location and photos among the connections you may grant.

None of this is forced: you can use Wabi with no connections at all, and connect services per thread, for a purpose.

The built-in safeguards (per the company)

  • Per-chat scoping. Access is granted per conversation, not globally. A chat only sees what you shared in that chat.
  • Transparency in shared chats. Everyone in a chat can see when someone's personal information is requested there — e.g. a partner asking “Is she free next Thursday?”
  • SOC 2 compliance. Wabi states its data-security controls are independently audited (SOC 2), data runs in the cloud and is stored mostly in the United States.
  • Approval before action. Per the official store descriptions, Wabi checks with you before important actions that require your approval, and you can disconnect connections at any time.

What the App Store privacy labels say

Apple requires developers to declare their data practices. Wabi's App Store listing declares: Data Used to Track You — Identifiers. Data Linked to You — User Content, Identifiers, Diagnostics. Data Not Linked to You — Identifiers. The listing also states the app is for ages 13+ and its declared language is English (checked October 20, 2026).

Who processes your data

Launch coverage notes Wabi routes work through AI models from Google, Anthropic and OpenAI. In practice that means content you connect or discuss may be processed by those providers' systems as part of answering you — standard for agent products, worth knowing before you connect sensitive accounts.

Sensible precautions

  • Start with no bank connection; add it later, in one thread, if a task truly needs it.
  • Keep sensitive topics in solo threads, not shared ones.
  • Periodically review which threads have which connections.
  • Remember group members see shared-thread content — don't paste secrets into a group chat.
  • Download only the real app — developer Wabi, Inc. (see the download guide).

Our verdict

Wabi's permission model is more thoughtful than most (per-chat scoping is genuinely good design), and the company is transparent about being audited. The honest caveat is simply that this is a brand-new product from a startup that hasn't settled its business model — enjoy the free assistant, connect accounts gradually, and keep anything truly sensitive out of it until it has a longer track record.
