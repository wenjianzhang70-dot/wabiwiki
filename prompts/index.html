Wabi AI Prompts Library

Copy any prompt below into a Wabi thread. Each one asks for a mini-app or action Wabi is built for — group planning, trackers, polls, lists and quizzes.

Group trips & events

Plan a 3-day trip to Tokyo for our group chat: build an itinerary we can all see, with a shared packing checklist. Make a poll so our group can vote on one of these five movies for Friday night. Create a countdown to our trip on June 5 and put it on my home screen.

Health & habits

Build a weight tracker in this thread and log my weight whenever I tell you. Create a habit tracker that fits my morning routine and checks in with me daily.

Family

Find kid-friendly events near us this weekend and keep the list updated. Quiz my daughter on Japanese vocabulary for 10 minutes, three times a week.

Couples & friends

Start a question of the day for me and my partner to answer together. Keep a list of restaurants we want to try and suggest one when we can't decide. Make a shared reading list for our book club with what to read next.

Home & daily life

Make a grocery list we can both add to from our phones. Give me a brief every morning with my top priorities from my email, calendar and to-dos.

Errands, bills & health

Watch my bills and subscriptions, and flag any bill that goes up or a subscription I'm not using. Keep an eye on my recent orders and flag any refund that's still missing. Summarise my week from Health Connect: steps, sleep and workouts, and how it compares to last week.

Tips for better Wabi prompts

  • One tool per request. “Build a poll” beats “plan my whole wedding.”
  • Say who it's for. Mention the thread or the people — group apps are Wabi's specialty.
  • Say where it should live. Ask for a home-screen widget if you'll check it daily.
  • Refine, don't restart. Ask Wabi to add a field, change a reminder time or share the app with the thread.

Prompts are examples written for this guide, based on Wabi's officially demonstrated capabilities as of October 2026. Results vary — Wabi generates each app fresh from your description.
