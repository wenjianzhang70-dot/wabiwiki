Wabi AI Mini-App Ideas

By Wabi Wiki Editorial · Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Quick steps

  1. Choose a category such as travel, health, family, couples and friends, or home and productivity.
  2. Pick an idea such as a trip capsule, weight tracker, family schedule board or shared grocery list.
  3. Describe the tool you want inside a Wabi thread, or use the wording in the prompts library.
  4. Treat the result as a starting point and refine it with Wabi as needed.

A mini-app is a small tool Wabi builds inside a chat thread — no app store, no code. The ideas below follow Wabi's own launch demos and official examples. Ask for any of them in your own words, or grab the exact wording from the prompts library.

Travel

  • Trip capsule: a 3-day city plan with a shared checklist and daily nudges.
  • Trip countdown: days-to-departure widget pinned to your home screen.
  • Group itinerary: one plan the whole group chat can see and adjust.

Health & habits

  • Weight tracker: log weigh-ins in your health thread, watch the trend.
  • Habit tracker: a daily check-in built around your actual routine.
  • Calorie & meal logs: simple trackers for everyday eating.

Family

  • Kids' events finder: kid-friendly activities near you, kept up to date.
  • Family schedule board: everyone's week in one shared view.
  • Language quiz: short daily quizzes for the kids (Wabi's founder demos a Japanese quiz for her daughter).

Couples & friends

  • Restaurant list: a running list of places you want to try together.
  • Live music tracker: gigs near you that match your taste.
  • Question of the day: one question a day to answer together.
  • Movie-night poll: group voting that ends the “what should we watch?” loop.
  • Shared reading list: books you're reading together, with what's next.

Home & productivity

  • Shared grocery list: everyone adds items; nobody buys duplicates.
  • Daily brief: your top priorities from email, calendar and to-dos in one morning view.
  • Countdowns & reminders: for deadlines, renewals and events.

Examples are drawn from Wabi's official site, its founder's launch walkthrough and launch coverage (October 2026). Wabi generates apps from your description, so treat these as starting points, not a fixed catalogue.
