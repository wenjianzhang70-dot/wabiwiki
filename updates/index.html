Wabi AI News & Updates

By Wabi Wiki Editorial · Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026

Short answer

Wabi 2.0 opened to everyone on October 19, 2026 on iOS, Android and Mac, free and with no waitlist. It followed an invite-only rollout that began on September 28, 2026, and the App Store shows iOS build 3.0.5 released on October 18, 2026. The log also tracks earlier app updates, Android and Mac availability, and the $20 million pre-seed announced on November 5, 2025.

A running, dated log of Wabi milestones, newest first. Every entry links to its source.

October 19, 2026 — Wabi 2.0 opens to everyone

Wabi's multiplayer AI assistant launched publicly on iOS, Android and Mac — free, no waitlist — after an invite-only period. Founder Eugenia Kuyda presented it as the realisation of the personal-agent concept she first pitched at TechCrunch Disrupt in 2014. Sources: TechCrunch, Unite.AI.

October 18, 2026 — iOS build 3.0.5

The App Store listing shows version 3.0.5 released two days before the public launch — the iOS build train (3.0.0 → 3.0.4 → 3.0.5) is moving weekly. Reminder: 3.0.x is the iOS build number; the product generation is Wabi 2.0 (see the FAQ).

October 14–15, 2026 — Mac app & Play listing update

Wabi posted Mac availability on October 14; the Google Play listing was updated on October 15, ahead of the public launch.

October 6, 2026 — Android availability posted

The company announced Android availability (the Play app, “Wabi: AI Messenger” by Wabi, Inc., is numbered 2.0.0).

October 1 & 5, 2026 — rapid-fire app updates

Two further app updates shipped in the invite period (iOS reaching 3.0.4 on October 5) — an early sign of the release pace.

September 28, 2026 — Wabi 2.0 invite rollout & the pivot

Wabi began rolling out version 2.0 by invite code: a shift from “type a prompt, get a mini-app” to a proactive personal assistant in a messenger, where the agent builds the apps and threads can be shared. The App Store labelled this update version 3.0.0. Sources: Unite.AI's launch report and the App Store version history.

November 5, 2025 — $20M pre-seed announced

Wabi announced a $20 million pre-seed round led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), alongside the first version of the product — a tool for creating and sharing mini-apps from text prompts. Source: TechCrunch (timeline).

This log covers major milestones only. Product details on this site reflect the October 2026 launch version.
