Wabi AI: The Complete Guide to the Multiplayer AI Assistant

Wabi AI is a free personal AI assistant you use together with other people. It lives in solo and group chats, organises your life into topic threads, and builds small interactive mini-apps — trackers, polls, itineraries, widgets — right inside the conversation. Wabi was created by Eugenia Kuyda, the founder of Replika, and opened to everyone on October 19, 2026.

Why people are searching for Wabi

Most AI assistants are built for one person in one endless chat. Wabi's bet is different: separate threads for separate parts of life, threads you can share with a partner, family or friends, and an assistant that turns talk into working mini-apps instead of walls of text.
